# Market-Portfolio
Making Portfolios for market

Equity Portfolio- For a 1 year investment horizon, and assuming 5 lakh initial cash. The code has been backtested for a 1 year horizon & the return is around 19% for 1 year(its showing around 11% as it selected Vedanta Limited but didn't accout for its demerger, because I have used yfinance). Even during this war like condition of March 2026 the max drawdown is approximately 9%.
Buying and selling is done on the closing prices for the day. Didn't account for taxes and charges as of now but will do so in future
